# cafe-commit
CET time zone French / English project debug and peer review space

## Our purpose

We are developers supporting developers. Our common purpose is to help one another through both the technical and personal difficulties of our craft.

Everyone's experience is valued, and no one is required to share.

Some of us bring deep expertise, others fresh eyes, but no one has all the answers. 

Together, we get closer to the right creative work or scientific truth, or simply remove a thorn from our side or shed light on a blind spot we didn't know was there.

We are volunteer-led and volunteer-supported. We have no paid staff and do not charge admission.

We are not a place for recruiting, selling, or self-promotion, and we are not a substitute for professional help.
