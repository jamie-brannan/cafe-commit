# project-cafe-commit-purpose-statement

We are developers supporting developers. Our common purpose is to help one another through both the technical and personal difficulties of our craft. 

There are no experts here, only peers. Everyone's experience is valued, and no one is required to share.

Some of us bring deep expertise, others fresh eyes — but no one has all the answers, and together we get closer to crafting the right creative work, scientific truth, or simply removing a thorn from our side, or getting light on blindspot we didn't know was there.

We are volunteer led and supported. We have no paid-workers, and do not charge admission. 

We not a place for recruiting, selling, or self-promotion, and we are not a substitute for professional help.
